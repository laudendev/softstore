Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

This Site uses a small number of cookies, all strictly functional — nothing here tracks you, profiles you, or is shared with any advertiser. We don't use Google Analytics or any similar service.

Cookies We Use

softstore_cart

Remembers the contents of your shopping cart between page loads, so items you've added don't disappear if you navigate around the Site or come back later. Contains a random identifier only — no personal information.

softstore_admin_session

Keeps a site administrator signed in after logging into the admin area. Only ever set for us, never for ordinary visitors browsing or purchasing from the Site.

Why We Don't Ask for Cookie Consent

Both cookies above are strictly necessary for the Site to function — a cart that forgets its contents, or an admin login that doesn't stay logged in, isn't really usable. Cookies that are strictly necessary for a site to work are generally exempt from consent requirements under cookie law, unlike tracking or advertising cookies, which we don't use.

Managing Cookies

You can block or delete cookies through your browser's settings at any time. Doing so may prevent items from staying in your cart between visits, or may sign an administrator out — but will not affect your ability to browse or complete a purchase.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy if the cookies we use ever change. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
